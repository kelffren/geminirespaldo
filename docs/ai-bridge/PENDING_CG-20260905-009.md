### PROBLEM
The hero is now visually scaled to 1.15x in `engine-ab.js` while keeping collider/physics unchanged, but `src/systems/armor-aura.js` still sized its ring, glow, particle orbit and sparks exclusively from `p.radius`. Since `p.radius` is the physical collider radius, the aura stayed at its old visual footprint while the body became larger. That silently re-coupled presentation quality to physics and made the 1.15x experiment visually inconsistent for players with aura ranks.

### CONFIRMED_IN_GEMINI
- Round base `main` was `5408c053fc54f0a85e5726ef84fbe5408bfda7a1`; `index.html` declares Kelo World V6.24.
- `engine-ab.js` remains the visible hero renderer and exposes `KELO_AVATAR_PRESENTATION` with `visualScale=1.15`, visual bounds, collider radius, foot root, depth root, shadow anchor and nameplate anchor.
- `engine-ac.js` remains the gait owner; stride still advances from actual world distance. This round does not alter gait, speed, input, frame selection, stop logic or camera.
- Before this round, `armor-aura-v1.0` used `(p.radius||20)` for ring radius, glow radius, particle orbit/rise and spark orbit. It did not read `KELO_AVATAR_PRESENTATION`.
- The live hero audit already runs mobile 390x844 DPR2 and desktop 1280x720 DPR1, and `live-audit.yml` triggers on `src/systems/**` and `scripts/live-hero-audit.mjs`.
- No new Grok locomotion feedback exists beyond `GC-20260831-003`.

### EXTERNAL_EVIDENCE
- MDN's 2D collision guidance treats the hitbox as a simplified collision shape rather than requiring pixel-perfect agreement with the visible entity. This supports preserving Kelo's collider while presentation/VFX grow separately.
- MDN documents `imageSmoothingEnabled=false` as the Canvas control used to keep scaled pixel-art images sharp, but this does not guarantee that fractional scale factors avoid temporal artifacts.
- W3C CSSWG issue #5837 and multiple engine/rendering issues document distortion/jitter/artifacts around non-integer or odd sprite scaling. Counterevidence: making dependent VFX follow the body scale improves proportional consistency but does not prove 1.15x itself is temporally pixel-perfect at every DPR/zoom.
- General VFX practice separates decorative effects from gameplay collision; the aura should therefore follow presentation size while collision authority remains `p.radius`.

### HYPOTHESIS
If aura geometry derives only its visual extent from `KELO_AVATAR_PRESENTATION.visualScale`, while retaining `p.radius` as the physical radius/anchor basis, the enlarged avatar will regain proportional aura coverage without any collision, movement or foot-root change.
