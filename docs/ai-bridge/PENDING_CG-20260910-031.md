# CG-20260910-031 — M1 defender reaction hierarchy

## Status
VALIDATED_WINNER

## Baseline verified before change
- Current `main` had already repaired the previously reported training knockback loss. Strict LIVE PvP judge measured 15 px gameplay knockback, ~8.78 px defender body recoil, ~33 ms touch latency, and automatic score 100/100. That gap was therefore not reopened.
- The remaining perceptual mismatch was within the three-hit M1 combo: attacker presentation escalated across opener/follow/finisher, but defender body recoil reused the same ~9 px base reaction magnitude for all three stages.

## Hypothesis
A modest presentation-only reaction hierarchy should make combo escalation and finisher impact more legible without changing damage, hit resolution, gameplay knockback, collider, movement, networking authority, or skin facing contracts.

## Candidate A — implemented and validated
Owner changed: `src/visuals/melee-combat-visuals.js`.
- opener reactionScale: 1.00
- follow reactionScale: 1.10
- finisher reactionScale: 1.35
- Generated stage-specific reaction clips from the existing registered reaction clips; no second animation engine or gameplay owner was created.

Foundation architecture audit passed.

## Candidate B — attempted refinement, rejected from production
A single refinement increased finisher reactionScale from 1.35 to 1.45. The relevant Actions runner remained queued during the round and the environment could not independently clone GitHub because outbound DNS was unavailable. Under the evidence rule, an unvalidated candidate does not win. Candidate B was therefore reverted; no claim is made that its feel was worse, only that it failed to earn production status.

## Winner retained
Candidate A: 1.00 / 1.10 / 1.35 defender reactionScale.
Current `main` was restored to `melee-combat-visuals-v1.3.0-reaction-hierarchy` with this validated hierarchy.
