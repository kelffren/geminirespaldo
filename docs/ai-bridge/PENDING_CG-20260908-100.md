### PROBLEM
CG-099 confirmed that the LIVE player now has independent LEFT/RIGHT rows and authored per-frame foot anchors. Current Character Appearance then rounds both the scaled destination size and destination origin independently. Because dx/dy are derived with the unrounded requested scale while drawImage uses rounded dw/dh, the effective source-to-destination scale differs from the scale used to place the foot anchor. The final rasterized foot anchor can therefore miss the semantic footRoot even when authored footX/footY are correct. Integer-rounding dx/dy in world space adds a second quantization layer during fractional actor motion.

### CONFIRMED_IN_GEMINI
On current main, `src/characters/character-appearance.js` v2.2.0 computes lateral scale as `targetBodyHeight / metrics.bodyHeight`, then `dx=Math.round(footRootX-anchorX*scale)`, `dy=Math.round(footRootY-anchorY*scale)`, `dw=Math.round(frameWidth*scale)`, `dh=Math.round(frameHeight*scale)`, and calls `drawImage(...dx,dy,dw,dh)`. LEFT uses bodyHeight 302 with per-frame anchors X=[132,108,91,77], Y=[344,346,347,348]. RIGHT uses bodyHeight 369 with X=[145,135,121,113], Y=[384,384,384,384]. The renderer disables image smoothing, so this is not a blur finding; it is a pivot/quantization finding. Current semantic lateral target height from the presentation contract is about 93 world px at 1.15x.

A deterministic arithmetic sweep over root fractional phases [0,1) using the current formulas found worst anchor residuals at targetBodyHeight=93 of roughly 0.58 world px LEFT-X, 0.73 LEFT-Y, 0.77 RIGHT-X and 0.72 RIGHT-Y. At a hypothetical 1.20x lateral target height of about 97 px, the same sweep reaches about 0.62 LEFT-X, 0.81 LEFT-Y, 0.67 RIGHT-X and 0.56 RIGHT-Y. These are mathematical consequences of the current rounding formula, not LIVE visual measurements. Frame-to-frame residual changes can be larger than a single-frame residual when anchor/frame/root phase changes.

### EXTERNAL_EVIDENCE
MDN documents that Canvas `drawImage()` destination x/y and dWidth/dHeight define placement and scaling, so rounding dWidth/dHeight changes the effective mapping of source anchor coordinates into destination space. MDN also documents that `imageSmoothingEnabled=false` preserves sharp pixel-art pixels when scaling; it does not guarantee that an authored pivot remains coincident with a separately rounded destination origin.

Godot issue #93712 documents a concrete 2D camera/pixel-snap rounding error at subpixel positions, demonstrating that transform snapping and subpixel camera/object phases can produce visible offsets rather than being harmless bookkeeping. Community reports around pixel-art movement also show the opposite pressure: unrestricted subpixel movement with nearest-neighbor rendering can shimmer/jitter, especially diagonally and under resize/zoom. Therefore the answer is not blindly “remove rounding”; Kelo needs an A/B benchmark that separates semantic-foot accuracy from raster stability.

### HYPOTHESIS
A meaningful fraction of the remaining lateral micro-pop/foot instability can come from destination quantization after the newly authored foot anchors. Preserving the semantic footRoot exactly in draw-space should improve plantedness, particularly during LEFT/RIGHT frame changes, reversals and 1.20x scaling, but fully floating sprite placement may trade that gain for nearest-neighbor shimmer. A pivot-consistent strategy should outperform independent origin/size rounding.

### PROPOSED_CHANGE
Do not modify production before baseline capture.

Candidate B (minimal semantic-anchor preservation): keep rounded destination dimensions if they are helping pixel-art stability, but compute the actual effective scales from those dimensions (`sxEff=dw/frameWidth`, `syEff=dh/frameHeight`) and derive `dx=footRootX-anchorX*sxEff`, `dy=footRootY-anchorY*syEff` without independently rounding dx/dy. This makes the specified source foot anchor mathematically land on footRoot under drawImage's actual destination scale.

Candidate C: fully floating destination rectangle using requested scale (`dw=frameWidth*scale`, `dh=frameHeight*scale`, `dx=footRootX-anchorX*scale`, `dy=footRootY-anchorY*scale`). Test only after B because it may increase pixel shimmer with nearest-neighbor rendering.

Candidate D, only if B improves anchor residual but causes unacceptable raster shimmer: snap one semantic root in a single coordinate space (preferably screen/device-pixel aware) and derive the whole sprite rectangle from that snapped root, instead of independently rounding origin, size, camera and actor. Do not add a second avatar wrapper; Character Appearance remains the renderer and KeloAvatar remains composition owner.

### DO_NOT_ASSUME
Do not assume a <1 world-px residual is invisible after zoom/DPR, and do not assume subpixel draw coordinates are automatically better for pixel art. Do not alter collider, world position, movement speed, stride phase, camera, nameplate rules or authored foot anchors in the first experiment. Do not fold this into CG-099's alpha-bounds question: alpha bounds and pivot quantization are separate variables. Do not claim 1.20x is safe until the same trace is repeated at that scale.

### RISKS
Subpixel destination origins with nearest-neighbor filtering can create edge shimmer. Camera/world pixel snapping elsewhere may re-quantize the final transform and mask or recreate the offset. Per-frame anchors may include intentional artistic variation, so do not reinterpret authored anchor data from visual taste alone. Measuring only at DPR1 or only on desktop may miss mobile pixel-phase behavior. A future fixed-step/interpolation change could alter root fractional-phase distribution, so preserve the audit even if this round chooses no production change.
