### PROBLEM
The latest main already fixed idle→movement onset: the first real resolved displacement steps off plant frame 2 immediately, with 60/90/120 Hz onset reduced to roughly 8–17 ms and blocked intent remaining planted. The next unresolved priority-1 issue is screen-space pixel phase during steady lateral/diagonal travel.

Current Character Appearance rounds destination x/y and destination width/height in WORLD coordinates before engine-c applies the world camera transform. KeloCamera/engine-h declare pixel-perfect/roundPixels behavior, but engine-c then renders with a potentially non-integer effective landscape zoom. This means integer world-coordinate rounding does not imply alignment to physical display pixels after zoom+DPR. It can quantize the actor relative to its continuous world position while still landing on fractional physical pixels, producing a candidate source of shimmer/judder around lateral frame changes or camera follow.

### CONFIRMED_IN_GEMINI
- main HEAD at research start: 7984503dffe4477366be4e22f66ab3b77349ffb8.
- GC-20260909-009 is IMPLEMENTED_VERIFIED. It changed only presentation onset and preserved movement/collision/shared client-server profile.
- `engine-h.js` calls `KeloCamera.configureViewport({ pixelPerfect:true, roundPixels:true, smoothing:false, imageRendering:'pixelated' })` and chooses base zoom through `pixelPerfectZoom(1)`.
- `KeloCamera.effectiveZoomFor()` leaves portrait at the base zoom but multiplies landscape zoom by `innerHeight/innerWidth`; therefore landscape effective zoom is normally non-integer even if portrait physical scale was integer-aligned.
- `engine-c.js` applies world rendering as `translate(screen center) → scale(effective zoom) → translate(-camera.x,-camera.y)`.
- `src/characters/character-appearance.js` computes raw world destination from semantic foot root and per-frame authored foot anchors, then executes `Math.round(...)` on dx, dy, dw and dh BEFORE the camera transform.
- `imageSmoothingEnabled=false` is correctly preserved around the sprite draw.
- Current `engine-ab.js` still declares avatar visual scale 1.15 and semantic foot/depth/shadow/nameplate separation.

Illustrative current math for a common 844×390 landscape viewport with DPR 3 and base zoom 1.0:
- effective zoom ≈ 390/844 = 0.4621.
- one 1-world-pixel rounding step becomes ≈0.462 CSS px or ≈1.386 physical px.
- a ±0.5 world-px position quantization error becomes up to ≈0.231 CSS px or ≈0.693 physical px for a single sample; frame-to-frame rounding-error swing can approach double that.
Thus `roundPixels=true` as a declared viewport policy is not enough to prove physical-pixel alignment for the avatar at landscape zoom.

### EXTERNAL_EVIDENCE
- Phaser 3/4 official camera documentation says sub-pixel target positions can cause slight jitter and that `roundPixels` can help, but explicitly warns that this can still break with a non-integer camera zoom. This is directly analogous to Kelo's landscape effective zoom policy.
- MDN confirms `imageSmoothingEnabled=false` controls filtering of scaled images, preserving hard edges, but it does not itself guarantee destination pixel-grid alignment.
- MDN's pixel-art guidance warns that non-integer scaling factors can produce undesirable sampling results.
- Community reports from pixel-art engines describe diagonal/camera shimmer surviving simple integer position rounding when camera scaling or fractional movement remains involved. Treat this as anecdotal corroboration, not proof for Kelo.

### HYPOTHESIS
The remaining visible lateral jitter is more likely to come from a mismatch between WORLD-space rounding and SCREEN/PHYSICAL-pixel phase than from stride timing itself. At portrait integer-aligned zoom, current rounding may be acceptable; at landscape non-integer zoom, it can create irregular screen-space steps even while world movement and stride are smooth.

A presentation-only screen-space snap of the avatar destination, performed after considering camera zoom and DPR and then converted back to world coordinates for the existing draw call, may reduce shimmer without changing physics, camera trajectory, collision, stride, aim or authoritative movement.

### PROPOSED_CHANGE
Do NOT implement immediately. First benchmark three presentation policies using the same real `CharacterAppearance` math:

A — CURRENT
- Round dx/dy/dw/dh in world coordinates as today.

B — NO WORLD POSITION ROUNDING
- Keep raw dx/dy world coordinates while retaining smoothing=false; measure whether browser rasterization is smoother or blurrier at current downscale.

C — SCREEN/PHYSICAL-PIXEL SNAP FOR POSITION ONLY
- Compute raw world destination.
- Project the destination to screen using the canonical KeloCamera transform.
- Snap screen x/y to `1 / DPR` CSS-pixel increments (physical pixel centers/grid as appropriate for the measured draw semantics).
- Convert snapped x/y back to world coordinates and draw there.
- Keep world size/collider/footRoot/stride unchanged.
- Do not force global camera zoom to integers and do not snap gameplay position.

Only test width/height edge snapping as a separate follow-up if position-only snapping fails; mixing position and size changes in the first experiment would obscure attribution.

### DO_NOT_ASSUME

- Do not assume no-rounding is better: with smoothing disabled and non-integer destination scaling it may create visible sampling shimmer.
- Do not assume integer effective zoom is acceptable globally; forcing it in landscape could materially alter framing and field of view.
- Do not touch movement speed, shared movement profile, collider, stridePhase, foot anchors, aim-facing or camera follow tuning in this pass.
- Do not add another renderer/manager.
- Do not move the shadow separately from the semantic foot root unless a later dedicated shadow benchmark proves a need.
