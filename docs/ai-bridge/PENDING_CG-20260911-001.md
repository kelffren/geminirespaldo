PROBLEM:
PvP camera followed the local player and had aim/action framing, but did not compose player+enemy context or adapt framing by distance/critical HP. Low HP had no progressive peripheral cinematic state tied to healing.

CONFIRMED_IN_GEMINI:
KeloCamera is the live camera owner and retains legacy follow internally. KeloPvPVisualCompetitivePass is presentation-only support loaded by the runtime bootstrap. HP is available on localPlayer and reconciled from authority; visual presentation may read it but must not become authoritative.

BASELINE:
No NORMAL/ENGAGED/CRITICAL director state. No two-fighter weighted framing. No distance-aware PvP zoom. No low-HP hysteresis or progressive heal fade. Existing PvP visual support used a fixed camera lead rather than contextual composition.

IMPLEMENTATION_A:
Added KeloCamera PvP director intent APIs and NORMAL/ENGAGED/CRITICAL presentation states. Director uses player as primary reference, nearest enemy as weighted secondary context, aim lead, distance-aware zoom and bounded impact micro-focus. Added screen-space critical overlay with continuous HP intensity, enter 20% / exit 25% hysteresis, fast damage response, slower healing response, peripheral vignette/pulse/edge displacement, and central stable zone.

AGENT_B_A:
PIERDE, score 8.64/10. Camera composition itself passed visibility and bounded-framing gates. Critical FX was too aggressive: maxVignette=.78 and edgeShakePx=3.2 exceeded competitive limits. Desktop sample also regressed to p95=26.7ms, p99=38.9ms with one frame >33ms.

FAILURE_FOUND:
Candidate A used stronger peripheral displacement and multiple inset shadow layers. The cinematic signal was readable but exceeded the intended control/readability/performance budget.

IMPLEMENTATION_B:
Kept the camera director because it passed all camera gates. Reduced critical maxVignette .78→.60, edgeShakePx 3.2→1.45, pulseHz 2.1→1.85, shockDecay 12→14, reduced shock modulation, and replaced the expensive multi-shadow overlay with one radial gradient plus transform/opacity. Healing damping and 20%/25% hysteresis were preserved.

AGENT_B_B:
GANA, 10/10 mobile landscape and 10/10 desktop, zero hard blockers and zero page errors. Both fighters remained visible. Critical state activated correctly. Overlay remained pointer-events:none and peripheral-only. Heal remained progressive rather than instant.

WINNER:
KeloCamera contextual PvP director from Candidate A + optimized Critical Health FX Candidate B.
