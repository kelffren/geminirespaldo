### PROBLEM

V6.09 now loads `src/systems/armor-aura.js` after the hero renderer and depth compositor. The aura is a presentation effect around the character, but all of its geometry is derived from `p.radius || 20`, which is the gameplay collider. The production hero sprite is independently rendered from a semantic foot location (`footY = p.y + 10`) at about 48x81 world px laterally. This couples a new visual effect to physics exactly when the current user priority is to enlarge the character while keeping collisions unchanged.

### CONFIRMED_IN_GEMINI

At baseline `ac1459b94991e3c8a700548227004d92dfc5f0bf` / visible V6.09:

- `localPlayer.radius` remains 20 in `engine-a.js`.
- `engine-ab.js` renders the production hero independently of radius:
  - lateral destination width `dw = 48`;
  - height `dh = round(54 * FH/FW)`; with the current 4x4 sheet assumptions this is about 81;
  - semantic foot location `footY = p.y + 10`.
- `src/systems/armor-aura.js` derives every aura component from the collider radius instead of hero presentation geometry:
  - ring center Y = `p.y + radius*0.72` = `p.y + 14.4` at radius 20;
  - ring radius X = `radius * (1.05 + rank*0.075) * pulse`;
  - body glow radius = `radius * (1.35 + rank*0.07 + pulse)`;
  - particles use radial extent and rise height based on `radius`;
  - rank-9 spark radius = `radius*1.45`.
- Therefore the ring is centered about 4.4 world px below the current hero `footY` before any bob/lean/scale work.
- At rank 9, ignoring the small pulse term, ring `rx` is about 34.5 world px. The current lateral half-width is 24 world px, so the ring is currently wider than the body. That accidental visual relationship is not preserved when the avatar is scaled because radius is intentionally invariant.
- Current particle vertical rise at radius 20 is about 46 world px (`2.3*radius`). The current lateral sprite top is roughly `p.y - 71`; therefore the particle field does not span the full visible body even before avatar enlargement.
- If the avatar presentation is enlarged uniformly to 1.25x while radius remains 20, hero lateral dimensions become about 60x101 while aura geometry remains unchanged. The effect-to-body ratio shrinks materially.
- `armor-aura.js` wraps `renderAvatar`, so every `renderAvatar` call also executes aura back/front rendering. This means the already-open duplicate actor-pass issue (CG-022) also multiplies aura draw work near compositor paths; this round does not re-open CG-022, but any aura benchmark must count passes.
- `ENGINE_MAP.md` is stale: it still identifies `engine-m.js` as the live hero renderer and does not describe the new armor-aura/equipment stack, while `index.html` V6.09 loads `engine-ab.js` and then `armor-aura.js`.

### EXTERNAL_EVIDENCE

- PixiJS documents `pivot`, `anchor`, and `scale` as presentation-transform concepts and distinguishes them from other object concerns. A child/effect can share an actor presentation transform while retaining its own local animation. This supports a visual root/presentation envelope instead of physics radius as the only sizing primitive.
- Godot `Sprite2D` exposes independent sprite offset/centering/frame geometry and a `get_rect()` visual rectangle, again supporting separation of visual bounds from physics geometry.
- A long-standing PixiJS issue discussing character + shadow composition explicitly treats character and shadow as separate visual objects that should move together under a shared parent while retaining independent animation.
- Community counterevidence: some effects are intentionally gameplay-radius indicators (attack range, aggro radius, hit radius). In those cases collider/range-derived geometry is correct. Kelo's armor aura, however, is decorative/rank presentation and currently has no gameplay-radius meaning.
- Godot community debugging guidance repeatedly distinguishes sprite alignment from collision shape alignment and recommends keeping physics centered/simple while moving visual presentation independently when needed.

### HYPOTHESIS

A scale-safe actor presentation contract should expose at least:

- `physicsRoot` / collider radius for gameplay only;
- `footRoot` for planting/depth;
- stable `visualEnvelope` (or presentation width/height) for body-relative VFX and occlusion;
- `presentationScale` for hero size experiments;
- optional VFX-specific scale multipliers independent of gameplay radius.

The armor aura should default to `footRoot + visualEnvelope` geometry, not `p.radius`, unless an effect explicitly represents a gameplay range.

### PROPOSED_CHANGE

Do not change production immediately. Benchmark the current aura against a presentation-derived candidate.

Candidate A — BASELINE_RADIUS:
- current `p.radius` sizing and anchors.

Candidate B — PRESENTATION_ROOT:
- ring center anchored to `footRoot` (initially current `p.y+10` contract);
- ring width derived from stable visual width/envelope, e.g. `visualWidth * auraWidthFactor(rank)`;
- vertical glow/particle extent derived from stable visual height/envelope;
- collider remains 20 and is never read as a visual body size.

Candidate C — HYBRID:
- footRoot anchor + a VFX base size that intentionally blends collider and visual envelope only if B proves visually excessive at low rank.

Do not combine this test with avatar bob, lean, shadow, sampling, or a new sprite atlas.

### DO_NOT_ASSUME

- Do not assume a larger aura is automatically better.
- Do not assume collider-based effects are always wrong; they are appropriate when the effect communicates physical/gameplay range.
- Do not change `localPlayer.radius` to make the aura match a larger sprite.
- Do not make aura geometry follow per-frame alpha bounds; that could make the effect breathe/jitter with animation frames. Use a stable envelope.
- Do not refactor duplicate actor passes as part of this experiment; only instrument aura draw count so CG-022 remains isolated.
- Do not mark scale-safe until mobile/desktop and zoom/DPR combinations are measured.

### EXPERIMENT

1. Use baseline V6.09 and fixed actor location away from fountain/plaza compositor complications first.
2. Evaluate aura ranks 1, 3, 5, 7, 9.
3. Evaluate hero presentation scales 1.00, 1.15, 1.25, 1.30 with collider fixed at 20.
4. For each scale/rank, capture idle, RIGHT WALK, RIGHT RUN, LEFT WALK, LEFT RUN, hard reversal.
5. Repeat representative viewport/zoom combinations: mobile ~390x844, mobile ~430x932, tablet ~768x1024, desktop ~1440x900; include current zoom policy.
6. Compare A vs B; test C only if B visually over-expands low-rank effects.
7. Then repeat one sample near a compositor/depth zone and record `renderAvatar`/aura draw counts to detect multiplication without changing render ownership.
8. Repeat motion sampling at 60/90/120 Hz to ensure foot-relative aura position is not advanced by render count or phase time beyond intended particle animation.

### DECIDING_METRICS

- `auraFootCenterOffsetWorldPx` and CSS px by zoom.
- `auraWidthToVisualBodyWidthRatio` by rank and avatar scale.
- `auraVerticalCoverageToVisualHeightRatio` by rank and avatar scale.
- `auraBodyRelativeCenterDriftPxP95` during RIGHT/LEFT/reversal.
- `auraDrawsPerActorPerRAF` and `uniqueAuraCTMCountPerRAF`.
- `frameTimeP50/P95/P99` with aura rank 0 vs rank 9.
- `particleCountPerActorPerRAF` and multiplied count near compositor paths.
- `colliderRadiusBeforeAfter` must remain exactly 20.
- collision trace, world trace, camera trace and stride trace must remain identical in presentation-only candidates.

Initial acceptance target for a presentation-derived candidate:
- body-relative aura ratios remain approximately stable as avatar presentation scale changes;
- foot-center drift does not grow with scale;
- no collider/collision changes;
- no unexplained frame-time regression;
- no additional render pass.

### RISKS

- A presentation-derived aura may become visually too large and obscure nearby actors/UI at high rank.
- Scaling particles with avatar height can increase fill-rate/visual noise even when particle count stays constant.
- If `visualEnvelope` is introduced prematurely without a stable foot-root contract, later avatar work may need another migration.
- Duplicate `renderAvatar` passes can multiply aura cost and confuse visual comparison; instrumentation must tag pass/CTM.
- `performance.now()` drives aura phase; this is fine for decorative motion, but duplicated render passes in one RAF can sample slightly different phase values unless phase is cached per frame/actor. Measure before changing.
